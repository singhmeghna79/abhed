Why we exist

The layer underneath the agent is the one nobody sells.

Every enterprise is going to run agents. The market sells that one way: a vendor's cloud, the vendor's model, your data leaving the building on every turn. A large part of the economy cannot buy it that way.

The problem

Banks, hospitals, defence, government, and anyone with a data-residency clause are offered a choice: wait, accept a weaker product, or build the whole harness themselves. Most are building. Most of it is the same thing, badly.

What Zybuu builds

The infrastructure that lets an organisation run AI workloads under its own control, at whichever tier it operates. Not a chatbot: the part that decides what a model may do, does it safely, records it, and can prove afterwards what happened.

Why Abhed first

An agent harness is that layer for agents. The model is a replaceable part; the harness is what an enterprise actually has to trust — and the one thing a platform team cannot buy off the shelf today.

7.80×harness > model variance

The scaffold around the model decides more than the model does. A controlled study on SWE-bench Verified found harness-induced variance exceeds model-induced variance by 7.80×, reversing the ranking in 6 of 9 model-pair comparisons. Most of the industry treats that layer as glue around someone else's API. Zybuu treats it as the product.

How Abhed competes

We don't compete on the model. We run theirs, or yours.

The incumbents own the developer who can accept their terms — and most regulated firms can, through a cloud region and a no-retention agreement. Abhed is for the ones that cannot: defence and intelligence contractors, sovereign and public-sector deployments, operational networks with no route out, and the banks whose policy is no cloud at all.
